PACS provide crop loans to farmers at concessional rates of interest as per RBI directives. The interest rate on crop loans is linked to the Repo Rate and is subject to a ceiling prescribed by the RBI. PACS also provide term loans for agricultural purposes such as land development, minor irrigation, farm mechanization, and purchase of dairy cattle.

The credit flow to PACS is channeled through the three-tier cooperative credit structure: State Cooperative Banks (SCBs) at the state level, District Central Cooperative Banks (DCCBs) at the district level, and PACS at the village level. PACS mobilize deposits from their members and non-members to supplement their lending resources.
