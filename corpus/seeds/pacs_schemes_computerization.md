The Government of India has launched the computerization of PACS project to modernize and improve the functioning of Primary Agricultural Credit Societies across the country. The project aims to bring all PACS on a common digital platform for online accounting, real-time reporting, and connectivity with DCCBs and SCBs.

Under the computerization project, PACS are being provided with hardware, software, internet connectivity, and training to staff. The centralized software solution enables PACS to maintain digital records of member data, loan accounts, deposit accounts, and inventory. This will facilitate faster loan processing, transparent accounting, and easier auditing of PACS operations.
