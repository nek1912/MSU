A Primary Agricultural Credit Society (PACS) is a basic unit and smallest co-operative credit institutions in India. It works on the grassroots level (gram panchayat and village level). PACS are registered under the Cooperative Societies Act of the respective state and function under the guidance of the State Cooperative Agriculture and Rural Development Bank (SCARDB).

The governance of PACS is managed by a Board of Directors elected from among the members. The Board elects a Chairman and Vice-Chairman from among its members. The day-to-day management is handled by a paid Secretary/Manager appointed by the Board. PACS are affiliated to District Central Cooperative Banks (DCCBs) which in turn are affiliated to State Cooperative Banks (SCBs), forming the three-tier cooperative credit structure.
