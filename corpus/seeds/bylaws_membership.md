section: "Membership"
---
Membership of a PACS is open to any person belonging to the village or area served by the society who is eligible and willing to accept the services of the society. Any individual who is a resident of the area of operation of the PACS and is engaged in agriculture or allied activities can become a member by paying the minimum share capital.

The liability of members is limited to the unpaid value of shares held by them. Every member has one vote in the general body meetings irrespective of the number of shares held. Transfer of shares requires prior approval of the Board of Directors. A member who ceases to be a resident of the area or who has not availed any service for a continuous period may be required to transfer or surrender shares.
