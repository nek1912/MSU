As of 27 June 2018, over 318 million bank accounts were opened and over ₹792 billion (US$12 billion) were deposited under the scheme. PMJDY accounts are linked to RuPay debit cards which provide accident insurance cover of ₹100,000 (enhanced to ₹200,000 for overdraft accounts) to the account holder. The RuPay card enables withdrawals at ATMs and payments at point-of-sale terminals across India.

According to the World Bank's Spring 2025 Poverty and Equity Brief, approximately 171 million people in India moved out of extreme poverty between 2011 and 2023, with the poverty rate declining from 16.2% to 2.3%. PMJDY significantly expanded financial inclusion by facilitating the opening of over 550 million bank accounts. As of March 2023, the Direct Benefit Transfer mechanism, supported by Jan Dhan accounts, had saved the government approximately ₹3.48 lakh crore in leakages.
